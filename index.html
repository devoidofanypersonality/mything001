About

I work across the 3D pipeline. Most of my work is character-focused, built in ZBrush, Blender, Maya and Marvelous Designer.

I also set up real-time characters for Unity and Unreal, including facial shape keys for face tracking, and write my own Blender addons for sculpting, animation and viewport tools.
